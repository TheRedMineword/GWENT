Frequently asked questions

How do I watch YouTube with no ads?

Paste any YouTube link into the box above and press Purify . You'll be taken to our clean player, which loads the video without ads, comments, or sidebar distractions. Works with regular videos, Shorts, and playlists.

What is the official Purify Video domain?

The official domain is purify.video . You may also see purifyvideo.com , which redirects here.

Is Purify Video the same as SkipVids?

Purify Video is maintained by SkipVids . Purify is the minimalist, paste-and-watch tool. SkipVids is the full YouTube no ads player with playlists, continue watching, background play, and more.

Does Purify Video block comments and sidebar distractions?

Yes. The player removes ads, comments, and the sidebar of recommended videos. You see only the content you came to see—nothing more, nothing less.

Does it support YouTube playlists?

Yes. Paste a YouTube playlist URL (one containing list= ) and the player will load the whole playlist in our clean interface.

Does it support SponsorBlock?

Yes. Sponsored segments are auto-skipped using the SponsorBlock community database. You can disable it from the player page if you prefer.

Does it preserve YouTube start times?

Yes. If your link includes ?t=60 , ?t=1m30s , or #t=42 , the player jumps to that timestamp. Share-friendly time-coded URLs work out of the box.

How do I use background play on mobile?

When you minimize the browser on iOS or Android, the video may appear in your notification panel with playback controls. Background playback varies by browser and device. For the most reliable experience, try the SkipVids browser extension .

Is my watch history private?

Yes. Your "Recently watched" list is stored only in your browser using localStorage . It never leaves your device. Clear it any time from the player page.

Does Purify Video store my data?

No accounts, no profiles, no server-side history. The only data is what your browser keeps locally for your own convenience.
